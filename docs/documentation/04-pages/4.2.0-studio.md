# 4.2.0 Page: Studio

## Overview
The Studio page introduces visitors to Kolkrabbi's design approach, services, and collaborative process. It showcases the studio's philosophy, service offerings, and provides context about the team and location in Reykjavík, Iceland.

**Route:** `/studio`
**Component:** `apps/web/src/routes/Studio.jsx`

## SEO Metadata
- **Title:** Studio — Kolkrabbi
- **Description:** Learn about Kolkrabbi's design approach, services, and collaborative process.
- **OG Title:** Our Studio
- **OG Description:** Discover our design philosophy and collaborative services.
- **OG Image:** `https://kolkrabbi.io/img/open-graph/open-graph-03.png`

## Page Structure

### Hero Section (`StudioHero`)
Establishes the studio's identity and focus.

**Content:**
- Eyebrow: "Kolkrabbi Vinnustofa"
- Title: "Modular design frameworks"
- Subheading: "Building visual systems"
- Body copy: "A small studio based in Reykjavík, focused on visual identity, custom typography, and design systems for brands."
- Background image: `/img/studio/tor-photo-13.png`

### Studio Overview (`StudioApproach`)
Detailed introduction to the studio's history and approach.

**Section label:** "Studio"
**Intro:** "Design studio and atelier, based in Reykjavík, focused on visual identities, branding, art direction, and design systems."

**Body paragraphs:**
1. "Kolkrabbi, founded in 2019, based in Reykjavík, Iceland and home to artist and designer Tór Grímsson, is a design studio and atelier focused on brand identity, UI/UX and graphic design for clients of all sizes."
2. "The studio creates customised solutions for brands looking to differentiate their visual presence in the eyes of their clientele."
3. "Brand identity with a story that reflects values, a message that aligns with audience, and a strategy to operate—tailored to the core of the brand."
4. "Visual language defined by foundational principles; from logo design and usage in various formats, to typography selection and style definition, color system and methodology behind brand palettes, to guidelines which document and communicate these systems and concepts."

### Services Section (`StudioServices`)
Overview of service offerings with navigation to relevant sections.

**Section label:** "Services"
**Intro:** "Visual identity, typography, and design systems."

**Service Cards:**
1. **Visual Identity** — "Brand strategy, logo design, and comprehensive visual systems that establish distinctive market presence." → `/work`
2. **Type Design** — "Custom typeface design and licensing for editorial, branding, and digital applications." → `/foundry`
3. **Design Systems** — "Scalable component libraries, design tokens, and documentation for digital products." → `/workshop`
4. **Creative Direction** — "Strategic guidance for visual campaigns, editorial projects, and brand evolution." → `/work`

**CTA button:** "View Our Work" → `/work`
