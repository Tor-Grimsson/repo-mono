## 10. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### General Characteristics
- **Professional but approachable**: Avoid jargon, use clear language
- **System-focused**: Emphasize structure, clarity, consistency
- **Educational**: Explain the "why" behind design decisions
- **Direct**: Get to the point without unnecessary embellishment
- **Confident**: Assertive without being boastful

### Example Patterns
- **Descriptive Titles**: "Custom typefaces & specimens" (specific, functional)
- **Value Propositions**: "Free & Open Source" (clear benefits)
- **Action-Oriented**: "Browse", "Explore", "View", "Launch" (active verbs)
- **Technical Precision**: "Variable Serif Typeface", "3-Axis Variable Sans" (accurate terminology)
