### **Artwork Details**

This is an **unframed archival art print**, produced using museum-grade materials and professional printing standards.

Each print is made to order, carefully inspected, signed, and shipped with protective packaging.

---

### **Edition**

- Limited edition
- **Never reprinted**
- Flat price within the edition
- Artist proofs exist and are **not for sale**

Once an edition is sold out, it will not be produced again in any size.

---

### **Materials & Print Process**

- Archival pigment ink (giclée)
- Museum-grade fine-art paper
- Acid-free, long-term archival materials

Designed for longevity and accurate reproduction of the original work.

---

### **Framing**

- Print is sold **unframed**
- Framing shown in images (if any) is for presentation only

This allows the buyer full control over framing and installation.

---

### **Shipping & Taxes**

- Shipping calculated separately at checkout
- Artwork is **VAT-exempt** (Iceland)

---

### **Paper Options**

Prints are produced on **museum-grade fine-art papers** from **Hahnemühle**, selected per artwork:

- **German Etching** — 100% cotton rag, textured matte surface
- **FineArt Baryta** — fibre-based baryta paper with higher contrast and depth

Paper choice is determined by the image characteristics and is stated on the Certificate of Authenticity.
