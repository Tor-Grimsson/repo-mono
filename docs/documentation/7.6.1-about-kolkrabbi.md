## Kolkrabbi Vinnustofa

Kolkrabbi, is a design studio and atelier, located in Reykjavík, focused on visual identities, branding, art direction, and design systems.

Whether a sleek website is needed, a meaningful visual identity, or specific user experience, Kolkrabbi has the expertise to bring that vision to life.

From concept to production, Kolkrabbi delivers exceptional work that exceeds expectations, while mindful of taking the time to understand projects needs in terms of scope, roadmap, and that deliverables are met on time, without issue!

## Kolkrabbi Studio (Variations)

### Version 1
Kolkrabbi, founded in 2019, based in Reykjavík, Iceland and home to artist and designer Tór Grímsson, is a full-service design studio and atelier focused on brand identity, UI/UX and traditional graphic design for clients of all sizes.

The studio creates customised solutions for brands that are looking to differentiate their visual presence in the eyes of their clientele.

### Version 2
Founded in 2019, Kolkrabbi is a creative design studio and atelier based in Reykjavík, Iceland. Home of artist and designer Tór Grímsson.

A solution based full-service design studio focused on brand identity, UI/UX and traditional graphic design. The studio creates customised solutions tailored to project specifics, through brand building, art direction, illustration, UI/UX, web development, and consultation for clients of all sizes.

Kolkrabbi partners with other experienced professionals to expand the studio's capabilities, allowing for precise solutions to our clients' needs.

### Version 3
Kolkrabbi, is a design studio and atelier, located in Reykjavík, Iceland founded by Tór Grímsson in 2019 to encompass project based freelance work.

Centred around client work, Kolkrabbi creates customised solutions tailored to project specifics, through brand building, art direction, and consultation.

Kolkrabbi offers a broad set of skills, such as; Brand design, using visual storytelling, typography, color theory, and market research as tools to build functional brand identities. Design for screens, web design and development, UI/UX, illustration and graphics for websites and social media. Design for print, from book and magazine layouts to reports and brochures, business cards, poster layouts, product packaging and preparation for production.

## Tór Guðmundsson / Tór Grímsson

### Short Version
Tór (Þórður, phonetically Tór-tór) is an Icelandic artist and the creative director of the studio. Working between digital and physical design, Tór's creative output ranges from pen and paper, digital and 3D. Tór's personal work has been featured in a variety of exhibitions and galleries, under various names, Svartval, Biskup, Konsulat.

### Full Version
Tór is an artist, designer, producer, and obsessive organiser—who when cosplaying as an adult designer, claims special focus on visual identity, design systems & illustration.

Born in Iceland—Tór holds a B.A. from IUA in design and visual communications, Bachelor in Fine Arts, diploma in Visual comms from Weissensee KHS in berlin, alongside Art theory studies at the University of Iceland.

He has worked in and around the creative industry for over 15 years, spanning multiple overlapping fields; starting with fine arts, exhibitions, music performances, and film production—that lead to design studies, publications, editorials, visual identities, UI/UX, and design systems—each year influencing the next.

Tór's Circadian work-rhythm is centered around Kolkrabbi, which is located in Reykjavík. Creating value for his clients through art direction, visual identities, branding and rebranding, template work, brand consultation and industry novelties.

When outside the studio, you'll find Tór making drawings or numbered prints under the pseudonym Biskup—and when times are especially high—making bangers, in the name of Konsulat.
